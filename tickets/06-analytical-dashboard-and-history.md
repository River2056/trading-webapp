# 06 — Deliver the complete analytical dashboard and histories

**What to build:** The operator can understand the current experiment and investigate past outcomes through a professional dark financial dashboard backed entirely by persisted projections.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Execute auditable risk-controlled paper trades; 05 — Close rounds, carry capital, and reset after bankruptcy.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The dashboard clearly shows paper-trading mode and running, stopped, or degraded/paused health.
- [ ] Initial, current, and available capital plus realized/unrealized/total profit use consistent NTD formatting and red/green semantics.
- [ ] Current cycle, current/completed rounds, and days since latest bankruptcy are displayed.
- [ ] Selected pairs, active strategy rules/versions, and frozen round risk settings are visible.
- [ ] Charts show equity, profit, exposure, and round-to-round performance with accessible labels and empty/loading/error states.
- [ ] Trade and round histories are paginated and filterable and expose enough provenance to explain outcomes.
- [ ] Browser journeys verify login, current dashboard, histories, positive/negative states, degraded status, and responsive presentation.
